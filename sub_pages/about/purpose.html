Our Purpose

Victory Baptist Church exists to glorify God by making disciples of Jesus Christ.
We do this in 3 ways:

1

"And let us one another provoke unto love and to good works: Not Forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner ofsome is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching."
- Hebrews 10:25"

2

"For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ: Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ:
- Ephesians 4:12-13"

3

"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen."
- Matthew 28:19-20"
