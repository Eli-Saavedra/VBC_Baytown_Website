Welcome To Victory Kids!

Starting with excellent nurseries, there is something special for every age child. For children three years old through sixth grade there are fun, friendly, and Bible-centered classes. These classes are staffed with trained, friendly, and well-qualified teachers and helpers who will make church exciting and memorable for your child.

Preschool

3-5 Year Olds

Your child will enjoy their small group Sunday school classes. Each class is divided by age and is specifically tailored for your child’s understanding. They will enjoy crafts, games, and a fun Bible lesson with other kids their age.

Every Sunday excited teachers are waiting for your child's arrival so they can share important truths from the Bible such as obedience, sharing, being kind, and best of all, who Jesus is!

Elementary

1st-6th Grade

Full of energy and excitement, your child will love their Victory Kids Rally. 1st-6th graders meet for a fun, themed rally with games, prizes, and a Bible lesson from God's Word.
