What is Faith Bible Institute?

Faith Bible Institute (FBI) is a through the bible course for every Christian. Students study chronologically through Every Bible Book & Doctrine in classes that meet only one evening each week. Over 58,000 Christians in over 1,400 churches across the US and on seven continents have enrolled in FBI's three year study. FBI combines verse by verse Bible teaching, student workbooks, and group classes to produce a life changing learning experience. Courses include six 17/18 week semesters (plus electives).

Course Level and Description

FBI Courses are on a college freshman & sophomore level. A High School diploma is not required to enroll. Current High School (9th-12th) Students may enroll no more than three years before their projected graduation date & must also be 14 by December 1st of the current school year. Many with advanced degrees enroll as well.

Faith Bible Institute offers courses covering every book of the Bible and every major doctrine of the Christian faith.
