WHO WE ARE

"The ministry of Victory Teens exists to glorify God by developing teenage disciples of Jesus Christ."

Each week our teens come together to deepen their faith, meet new friends, and create lasting memories. Our youth staff and volunteers are committed to helping you navigate your teen years with biblical courage and wisdom.

WHEN WE MEET

Teen Bible Class

Sundays at 10:00 AM

Youth Action Night

Wednesday at 7:00 PM

Meet new friends and ministry leaders for weekly studies of practical truths from the Bible and learn how you can become the man or woman God created you to be.

Parents

The teen ministry of Victory Baptist Church is focused on the whole family!

Through regular meetings throughout the year, parents of teens are given practical instruction and insight on how we can work together to see God work in teens life.

If there is a specific question or problem you face as the parent of a teenager, please call us at (281) 421-4862 , and we would be happy to do our best to help.
