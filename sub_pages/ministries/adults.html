Find Perspective

There's nothing worse than trying to find your way around in the dark! It can be hard to find your way in life, but God knows where you are and where you need to be.

Adult Bible Classes help you bridge that gap with a Biblical perspective on life.

Connect With Others

It's easy to feel like we are alone in sometimes, but God never intended for us to have to go through life alone.

Our Adult Bible Classes are designed to help you meet people in similar life situations as you, so that you can realize you are not alone on your journey.

Learn God's Word

There is nothing more critical to a successful Christian life than the Bible. It is our compass and the only way to know the heart of God.

Each class exists to equip you with the tools you need to discover and apply God's truth in your life.
