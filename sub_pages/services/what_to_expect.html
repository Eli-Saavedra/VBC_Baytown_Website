WHAT TO EXPECT

We know visiting a church can be intimidating.
Here are 6 things to expect during your visit.

Expect

A Warm Welcome

We love having guests at Victory Baptist Church, so be prepared to be greeted by our church family!

You will find our people friendly, welcoming, and ready to do their best to make you feel right at home.

Expect

A Connection Card

When you arrive for the service, you will be given a bulletin with a connection card inside by one of our greeters.

This card helps us have a record of your visit and allows you to have an opportunity to communicate any prayer requests, need, or questions you may have.

Expect

A Thank You Gift

Make sure you stop by the Guest Service table in lobby before you leave.

We have a special gift there that we love to give all of our guests as our way of saying “Thank you for joining us.”

Expect

Uplifting Music

Our Music team is dedicated to leading our Church in worshipping our God.

Expect to have your heart stirred and prepared to hear from the Word of God.

Expect

A Helpful Message

We believe that God’s Word should be at the center of all that we do.

Our Pastor's preaching will be straightforward and directly from God's Word. You can be confident that you will leave with something to take home and implement in your life this week!

Expect

An Invitation To Return

This may be your first time visiting Victory Baptist, but we certainly hope that it won’t be your last.

Don’t be surprised if someone encourages you to come again or invites you to another gathering. We want you to know that you are welcome to join us any time you are able.
